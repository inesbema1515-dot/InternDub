07 — Behind the project

How it works.

Dublin internships are spread across dozens of boards and careers portals, each with its own deadlines and fine print. Most of my friends found roles after they'd closed, so I built one board that updates itself and tells you straight away if your year can apply.

01

Scan

Every morning an AI agent searches LinkedIn, Indeed, gradireland, iAgora, The Inroad and company careers pages.

02

Clean

Each role is mapped to one schema: field, type, location, eligibility code, deadline, first-seen date, source.

03

Ship

The dataset is validated and republished as JSON beside this static page. IDs never change, so saved roles stay put.

04

Match

Filtering, eligibility matching, swiping and tracking all run in the browser, with progress kept on your device.
