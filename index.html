The build

Why I built InternDub.

The problem

Opportunities are scattered

Dublin internships are spread across dozens of job boards and career portals, each with different deadlines and small print about who can apply. Most of my friends found roles after they'd closed.

The solution

One board that updates itself

  • Every Dublin internship in one searchable feed
  • Year-of-study matching on every role
  • Deadline reminders, calendar export and sharing
  • Swipe mode, compare and an application tracker
  • Scholarships, hackathons and market insights
How it works

From 40 pages to one feed.

1

Collect

An AI agent scans job boards and company career pages at 06:43 every day.

2

Clean

Each role is sorted into one format: sector, type, location, who can apply, deadline.

3

Publish

The updated data goes live on the site automatically, with saved roles kept in place.

4

Match

Filters, matching, swiping and tracking all run instantly in your browser.
